FAQ

Questions buyers ask us.

Something not covered here? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Are the layouts approved?
Yes. Both layouts are approved by AHUDA, the Anantapur Hindupur Urban Development Authority. Golden Tridhi Farms carries L.P. No. 2/2025/1107/ATP/DPMS. Our team shares approval copies before booking.
Are bank loans available?
Yes, with SBI, HDFC, LICHFL and all national banks. Ask about loan support.
What amenities does Akshay Golden Gate have?
Club house, swimming pool, gym, indoor games, function hall, guest rooms, cafeteria, shuttle court, cricket pitch, kids play area and walking track. See the project.
Can I buy a plot at Golden Tridhi Farms and build later?
Yes. You can buy a plot on its own, or choose a plot with a ready farm house. Compare both options on WhatsApp.
How do I schedule a site visit?
Pick a project, date and time in the site visit planner, or call +91 77799 92688. Our team confirms the slot with you.
Where can I see prices and unit sizes?
Villa sizes and plans are on the Akshay Golden Gate page, and the plot-wise area statement is in the Golden Tridhi Farms brochure. Prices change as units are booked, so our team shares the current sheet directly. Request it on WhatsApp.
I live outside Anantapur. Can I see the project remotely?
Request a video walkthrough on WhatsApp and our team will arrange a time.

Specifications

What goes into the walls.

From the project specification sheet.

Doors and windows
Main door in teak wood with deco polish. Internal doors 8 feet high with hardwood frames and flush shutters in natural veneer, or engineered factory-finished frames and shutters. UPVC sliding glass doors and casement windows with premium hardware.
Electrical
Concealed wiring with PVC insulated copper wires of reputed make. Modular switches with sufficient power outlets and light points. Individual earthing system.
Flooring
Vitrified tiles in bedrooms, foyer, living, family and dining areas, with granite tops on counters. Vitrified or ceramic tiles for toilet floors and walls up to lintel height. Anti-skid flooring for balconies.
Toilets
Wall hung water closets, wash basins with premium basin mixers and shower heads. Hydro-pneumatic water supply for toilets and kitchen. Water efficient sanitaryware and CP fittings. Exhaust fan provision.
Services
CAT6 cabling in all rooms for phone and internet. Wi-Fi provision. Round the clock security. STP water treatment plant. Solid waste management. All roads BT.
